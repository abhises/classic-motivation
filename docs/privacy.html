Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Classic Motivation is a personal tool used only by its owner. When you connect a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account it stores the access token and your display name locally on the owner's computer, so it can upload videos you choose to post. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.

Data requested from Google: permission to upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's name and ID (youtube.readonly). It is used only to post videos to your own channel.

Data requested from TikTok: basic profile info (user.info.basic) and permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish). It is used only to post videos to your own account.

Disconnecting an account on the Accounts page deletes its stored token. You can also revoke access in the platform's app settings at any time, for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
